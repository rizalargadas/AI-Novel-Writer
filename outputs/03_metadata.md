# Story Metadata

## Target Audience
Readers who enjoy shifter romance with slow-burn chemistry, competence-driven protagonists, political pack drama, and a fresh subversion of Alpha/Beta dynamics.

## Short Story Description
A **M/M shifter romance** set in a tense, post-succession **pack territory** where power is unstable and emotions run high. The story follows **Finn, a beta in his late 20s to early 30s**, a sharp-minded, quietly capable aide who becomes the emotional and political backbone of the pack while helping a volatile new Alpha hold everything together. As duty turns into desire, Finn and Theo must learn that real leadership is built on trust, not rank.
